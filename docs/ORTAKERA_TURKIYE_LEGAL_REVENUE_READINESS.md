# Ortakera — Türkiye Legal, Revenue & Operating Readiness Pack
## Founder working brief for Turkish counsel and launch planning
**Status:** Prepared for professional review. This is not legal, tax, immigration, accounting or licensing advice.

## 1. Founder objective
Prepare Ortakera to generate lawful first revenue before committing to a full permanent office and large payroll. Do not use revenue-first sequencing to evade a legal requirement: if a Turkish legal entity, permit, licence, tax registration, local representative or other authorisation is required for a specific activity, that activity must wait until the requirement is satisfied.

## 2. Business definition
**Company/project:** Ortakera Systems  
**Platform:** Ortakera Opportunity Network  
**Product:** Ortakera Connect  
**Evidence layer:** WorkProof Ledger  
**User journey layer:** TürkiyePass (proposed; not yet implemented)  
**Regional resource layer:** Regional Opportunity Intelligence (proposed; not yet implemented)  
**Promise:** Turn local potential into verified opportunity.

Ortakera is planned as a digital coordination platform connecting employers, candidates, authorised recruitment partners, accommodation providers and local service businesses in Türkiye. It is not a government authority, immigration law firm, accommodation certifier, investment platform or licensed employment agency unless and until the relevant authorisation is obtained.

## 3. Proposed revenue sequence
### Phase 0 — before taking money
- Confirm which contracting entity may lawfully sell each service, where it must be established and whether the founder can operate from their current country.
- Obtain Turkish counsel's written activity-by-activity licensing analysis, especially for recruitment, job placement, candidate introductions, accommodation intermediation and marketplace activity.
- Obtain a Turkish SMMM (licensed certified public accountant) view on tax registration, invoicing, VAT, withholding, payroll, permanent establishment, cross-border receipts and accounting.
- Do not accept money for an activity until the contracting party, tax treatment, consumer terms, refund/cancellation rules and required permissions are clear.

### Phase 1 — first commercial offer (preferred starting point)
Sell a narrowly scoped B2B service to businesses, subject to counsel's approval. Candidate options for counsel to classify:
1. Employer-facing multilingual customer-support / guest-communication operations (service delivery, not recruitment).
2. Employer vacancy intake, job-description structuring and workflow software, without introducing, selecting or placing candidates unless authorised.
3. Business onboarding and operational coordination software subscriptions.

Preferred first paid pilot: a defined business-service or software workflow with named deliverables, a fixed scope, clear acceptance criteria, written contract, invoice and payment terms. Do not sell a vague promise of access to candidates, jobs or permits.

### Phase 2 — partner-enabled employment
Only after counsel confirms the model: work with an İŞKUR-authorised private employment agency under a written agreement that defines who advertises, screens, introduces, places, contracts and invoices. Ortakera must not imply that a partner's licence automatically authorises Ortakera to perform regulated activities itself.

### Phase 3 — accommodation and local services
Begin with provider intake and non-transactional enquiry/coordination only if counsel approves. Add paid listing, booking commission, payment collection or accommodation intermediation only after checking the applicable tourism, rental, consumer, e-commerce, tax and local-authority rules.

### Phase 4 — broader TürkiyePass and ROI
Expand to regional experiences, workspace, longer stays and local providers after category-specific permissions, provider evidence, liability allocation and operational capacity are established. Tokenised property/agriculture investments and payment custody remain out of scope.

## 4. First revenue model to test
Prioritise employer and business customers; do not charge job seekers recruitment or placement fees.
- **B2B service pilot:** one defined business outcome, fixed deliverables, time-boxed pilot, upfront or milestone billing.
- **Employer software/workspace:** subscription only for features actually delivered and legally permitted.
- **Corporate guest operations:** multilingual guest communication / reservation-support services, subject to service and data-processing contracts.
- **Partner operations:** software or administrative workflow support to properly authorised agencies; no disguised placement fee or unlicensed recruitment.

Do not publish prices, conversion forecasts or revenue projections until delivery cost, tax treatment and customer evidence are known. Measure paid conversion, gross margin, time-to-deliver, repeat purchase and refunds/disputes.

## 5. Turkish lawyer engagement — requested mandate
Find a Turkish lawyer or law firm with demonstrable experience in (a) foreign founders and company formation, (b) employment/recruitment agency regulation and İŞKUR licensing, (c) technology/platform and e-commerce regulation, and (d) data protection and cross-border processing. A general company-formation service alone is not sufficient.

Ask the lawyer to provide a written opinion or scoped memo answering the questions in Section 6, with the relevant statute/regulation, regulator, responsible entity, required permission, expected sequence, dependencies and a list of unresolved facts. Ask for a fixed-fee initial assessment and separate quotes for incorporation and ongoing retainer. Verify the lawyer's current bar registration and obtain an engagement letter defining scope, conflicts, confidentiality, deliverables, fees, taxes, expenses and termination.

## 6. Questions for Turkish counsel
### A. Founder, ownership and entity
1. Can the founder (a Nigerian national / non-Turkish resident, if applicable) own 100% of the proposed Turkish limited company? Are there restrictions for any planned activity?
2. Should Ortakera use a Turkish limited company (Ltd. Şti.) or joint-stock company (A.Ş.) at launch, considering capital, governance, investment, future partners and liability?
3. What minimum paid/subscribed capital and timing apply on the intended incorporation date? What changes in 2026, if any, must be considered?
4. Which documents must be apostilled/legalised and translated in the founder's country? Is a Turkish tax number, power of attorney, local address or in-person visit needed?
5. Can the founder act as shareholder and/or manager from abroad? If the founder travels to Türkiye to work, manage daily operations or serve as director, what work permit or exemption is required?
6. Can the business begin from a registered virtual office or serviced address, or is a physical office required for any activity, licence or work permit?

### B. Recruitment and job platform
7. Which precise activities constitute private employment agency activity under Law No. 4904 and related regulations: job listings, employer subscriptions, CV storage, matching, introductions, candidate screening, interview scheduling, referrals, placement, overseas recruitment and recruitment-agent marketplace?
8. Which of these activities require an İŞKUR private employment agency authorisation? What eligibility, capital, security/deposit, office, qualified staff, experience, fee and reporting conditions apply at the current date?
9. Can Ortakera lawfully provide software, advertising, candidate profile hosting or employer workflow services without being an employment agency? Where is the boundary in practice?
10. Can Ortakera contract with an İŞKUR-authorised agency as a technology provider or marketing supplier? What must the authorised agency control and sign off?
11. What restrictions apply to fees charged to candidates, foreign recruitment, cross-border service contracts, job advertisements and employer-paid fees?
12. What claims can Ortakera make about JobProof? How must it describe checks against official sources without implying government certification or a guarantee?

### C. Accommodation and local marketplace
13. Does listing, advertising, referring, arranging or taking commission on accommodation trigger real-estate brokerage, tourism, short-term rental or other licensing requirements?
14. What authorisation is required for short-term tourist accommodation, and who must hold it: property owner, operator, platform or intermediary?
15. What platform obligations arise from accepting bookings, collecting payments, setting cancellation terms, displaying prices, handling complaints or ranking providers?
16. Which experience categories (wellness, health, heritage, rural/agricultural visits, guides, transport) require licensed operators, qualified professionals, insurance or local permits?

### D. E-commerce, consumer, tax and payments
17. Is Ortakera a service provider, intermediary service provider, electronic commerce marketplace or a combination under Law No. 6563 and secondary rules? Does ETBİS registration or other notification apply?
18. What pre-contract disclosures, distance-sales terms, consumer withdrawal/cancellation/refund, complaint, advertising, commercial-message and electronic communication rules apply?
19. Which tax registrations, VAT treatment, e-invoice/e-archive obligations, withholding, corporate tax, transfer pricing and cross-border service export rules apply to each revenue stream?
20. Can the company receive payments directly? Must a licensed payment institution or bank handle marketplace funds, split payouts or escrow-like functions?

### E. Data protection, technology and contracts
21. For Turkish candidates, employers, providers and visitors, when does KVKK apply? Who is data controller/processor for each workflow? Is VERBİS registration required or an exemption applicable?
22. What legal basis, notices, retention periods, access controls, deletion process, breach response and data-subject rights are required?
23. What rules govern transferring personal data from Türkiye to hosting, analytics, support or AI providers outside Türkiye? Which standard contract, safeguards or notifications are needed?
24. What must be in the privacy notice, cookie policy, candidate/employer/provider terms, partner agreement, data-processing agreement, listing terms, booking terms and complaints/redress policy?
25. What limits apply to automated matching, profiling, ranking, sensitive data and AI-assisted translation or customer support?

### F. People, office and insurance
26. What employment contracts, payroll, SGK registration, occupational health and safety, workplace rules, social security and payroll tax apply to the first Turkish employee?
27. Can a foreign founder hire Turkish staff before obtaining a personal work permit, and what management activities may the founder perform from abroad?
28. What insurance is advisable or mandatory: professional liability, cyber, employer liability, public liability, accommodation/experience cover?
29. What records and licence conditions must be maintained at the registered office?

## 7. Initial staffing plan — lean, not a full office
### Hire 1: Operations & Provider Verification Coordinator (first employee when volume justifies it)
Own provider onboarding, document completeness, follow-up, listing freshness, case routing, audit discipline and customer support. This role does not make legal decisions, approve immigration eligibility, certify property safety or override a licensed reviewer.

### Hire 2: B2B Partnerships & Sales (after first paid pilot or repeatable pipeline)
Own employer discovery, qualified business leads, demos, proposals, contracts and renewals. Initially founder-led sales is preferable; do not hire a salesperson before a defined offer and target customer exist.

### Hire 3: Multilingual Content & Growth (fractional/contract first)
Own accurate Turkish/English content, regional provider education, SEO, social channels, campaigns and conversion analytics. Add other languages with qualified review. No fake listings, fake reviews, misleading visa claims or paid-job promises.

### External specialists from day one as needed
- Turkish lawyer (corporate, recruitment licensing, platform/e-commerce and data protection).
- Turkish SMMM accountant/tax adviser.
- Qualified translation/localisation reviewer for Turkish and any legal-facing content.
- Security/privacy engineer for production launch review.
- Authorised recruitment agency partner if employment placement is in scope.

Do not rent a permanent office or hire a large marketing team before recurring revenue. Start with remote work and a flexible/serviced workspace only if counsel and licensing rules permit it.

## 8. Production launch gates
**Gate 1 — Legal classification:** written activity matrix and permissions for each revenue line. No regulated service is sold before approval.
**Gate 2 — Commercial offer:** one defined B2B offer, contract, scope, price, invoice path, delivery owner, acceptance criteria and refund/complaint route.
**Gate 3 — Product safety:** production authentication, role permissions, secure secrets, backups/restore test, monitoring, rate limits, abuse handling, privacy controls, retention/deletion, audit logs and incident response.
**Gate 4 — Data integrity:** no synthetic live jobs/providers/reviews; clear source and review timestamps; expiry of stale evidence; human review for consequential decisions.
**Gate 5 — Customer operations:** support contact, response target, service terms, onboarding checklist, complaint escalation and documented service delivery.
**Gate 6 — Revenue proof:** first paid pilot completed and accepted; delivery cost and gross margin measured; repeat purchase or renewal evidence; no unresolved legal/compliance blocker.
**Gate 7 — Incorporation/scale decision:** choose the appropriate Turkish entity and office/staff footprint with counsel and accountant based on the approved operating model. If law requires incorporation before Gate 6 for the intended transaction, incorporate earlier.

## 13. Founder-specific operating assumptions — confirmed by founder
These are founder decisions for counsel to assess; they are not legal conclusions or evidence that the structure is already established.

- **Ownership intention:** the founder intends to own the Turkish business personally as an individual, subject to counsel confirming eligibility, documentation and the correct entity form.
- **Initial operating arrangement:** remote-first, with the founder initially coordinating from Nigeria. Do not assume that remote management removes Turkish company, tax, work-permit, permanent-establishment, data-protection or local licensing obligations.
- **Initial customer markets:** both Türkiye-based businesses and international businesses. Counsel and the SMMM should separately map contracting party, place of supply, VAT/invoicing, consumer/business status, data transfers and any local registration for each lane.
- **Founder role:** distinguish shareholder ownership from daily management, director/manager duties, signing authority, employment and physical work in Türkiye. Obtain written advice for the actual proposed role and travel pattern.
- **No nominal management:** do not appoint a paper manager or give a local representative authority they do not genuinely exercise. Any manager must have a real mandate, defined authority, records access, fiduciary responsibilities and documented limits.
