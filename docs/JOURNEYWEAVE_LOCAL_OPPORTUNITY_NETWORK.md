# JourneyWeave — Local Opportunity Network
**Company:** Ortakera Systems  
**Platform:** Ortakera Opportunity Network  
**Status:** Architecture implemented behind a default-off legal-hold control. Deployment does not activate commercial operation.

## Purpose
JourneyWeave extends Ortakera from work opportunity coordination into evidence-aware discovery of locally delivered experiences and services. It learns from hotel-led distribution and referral economics, but does not copy another operator's brand, interface, copy or commercial claims.

## Ortakera terminology
- **JourneyWeave:** cross-service discovery and enquiry journey.
- **PartnerReach:** approved partner referral channels.
- **LocalValue Exchange:** approved provider offers and service enquiries.
- **TrustTrail:** review state, reviewer, scope, timestamps and availability freshness.
- **Regional Opportunity Intelligence:** region/category discovery with transparent filters.

## Included in this implementation
- PostgreSQL-backed partner channels, local offers and service enquiries.
- Partner-only offer submission and provider-owned offer listing.
- Admin review workflow with explicit review notes and audit events.
- Public discovery only for active offers approved for scope, from active providers, with provider-confirmed availability in the preceding 24 hours.
- Customer enquiry capture with consent, locale, requested timing, offer reference and partner attribution.
- Partner workspace and public discovery page.

## API
- GET /api/v1/discover?category=&location= — public eligible offers.
- POST /api/v1/discover/enquiries — consented enquiry, not a booking.
- GET /api/v1/partner/channel — retrieve/create partner referral channel.
- GET /api/v1/partner/offers — partner's own offers.
- POST /api/v1/partner/offers — submit offer for review.
- PATCH /api/v1/partner/offers/:id/availability — provider refreshes availability.
- GET /api/v1/partner/requests — enquiries for that partner's offers.
- GET /api/v1/admin/local-offers — admin review queue.
- PATCH /api/v1/admin/local-offers/:id — review decision and mandatory review notes for approval/decline/suspension.

## Trust and privacy controls
- Partner routes require a signed-in, active account whose account type is partner.
- Offer ownership is enforced in SQL for provider operations.
- New offers default to submitted and unknown availability; never publicly visible at submission.
- Public visibility requires approved_for_scope, active provider, recently_confirmed availability, and a confirmation timestamp less than 24 hours old.
- Enquiry creation rechecks offer eligibility in the transaction, so a stale/withdrawn offer cannot be enquired against.
- Partner attribution is captured using an active channel code.
- Consent is mandatory and versioned. No payment-card, passport, permit or identity-document fields are collected.
- Enquiry response is explicit: an enquiry is not a reservation, confirmed price, or availability guarantee.
- Admin decisions are recorded in admin_audit.

## Deliberate boundaries
This release does not implement booking confirmation, inventory locking, calendar synchronisation, payment capture, refunds, supplier payouts, hotel integrations, automated government verification, accommodation brokerage or regulated travel packages. These require separate legal classification, supplier agreements, operational processes, security review and acceptance tests.

The 24-hour freshness threshold is a conservative initial software rule, not proof of live inventory. Providers must reconfirm directly with customers before making a commitment.

## Acceptance gates
1. CI syntax/unit and browser checks pass.
2. PostgreSQL integration verifies migrations, partner ownership, review transitions, stale-offer exclusion, enquiry consent, attribution and audit events.
3. Admin workflow reviewed with test accounts.
4. Public and partner pages tested at mobile and desktop widths.
5. Production database target confirmed and backup/restore state reconciled before any deployment.
6. Turkish counsel reviews accommodation/tourism intermediation, consumer/e-commerce, messaging, tax and data protection scope before commercial transactions.
7. No paid booking/commission is advertised or collected until payment, refunds, disputes and legal requirements are approved.

## Commercial learning to test
- Partner channel activation and attributed enquiry share.
- Provider response and handover time.
- Enquiry-to-provider-confirmed outcome rate (reported, not inferred).
- Availability staleness and correction rate.
- Repeat provider activity and willingness to pay for a clearly scoped business service.
- Direct operating cost per completed enquiry.

Do not present partner counts, GMV, revenue or conversion as traction until generated by real production records and reconciled evidence.

## Legal hold deployment mode
The environment flag JOURNEYWEAVE_ENABLED is OFF by default. Unless its exact value is "true", public discovery, partner APIs and customer enquiry submission return HTTP 423 with code LEGAL_HOLD. The public pages are preview-only and partner controls are disabled. Existing Ortakera employment and member routes remain independent. Admin evidence review may be used for internal technical preparation, but it cannot override the backend hold.

Do not set JOURNEYWEAVE_ENABLED=true until Turkish legal counsel, data protection review, supplier contracts, insurance/liability allocation, cancellation/refund and complaint handling, support coverage, payment controls, tax treatment and operational acceptance are signed off. Activation requires a separate change approval and end-to-end tests in a non-production environment.
