PRIVACY NOTICE · ENQUIRIES

How we use your enquiry

When you submit the Ortakera enquiry form, we collect the name, email address, optional organization, selected enquiry category, message, interface language and the time consent was recorded.

Purpose

We use these details only to receive, review and respond to the enquiry and to maintain a record of the communication. Submission does not create an employment relationship, service contract, partnership or account.

Storage and access

Enquiries are stored in the Ortakera application database. Access to the database and operational systems is restricted to authorized operators. Do not submit passwords, identity documents, financial account details, health information or other sensitive personal data through this form.

Retention and requests

We retain enquiry records only as long as needed for the stated communication, operational security and applicable legal obligations. To ask about an enquiry record or request correction or deletion, contact the Ortakera team using the contact channel published on the website and include the enquiry reference where available.

Important status

Ortakera is an opportunity coordination platform in development. This notice describes the enquiry form's current processing purpose; it is not a claim that Ortakera has completed all legal or regulatory compliance reviews in every jurisdiction.
