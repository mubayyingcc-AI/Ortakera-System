04OUR EMPLOYMENT STANDARDS

Trust must be
built into the process.

We will not build a marketplace around unverified promises. Every employer, role and employment pathway must meet the relevant checks before it is presented as an available opportunity.

01

Verified employer

Identity, business details and authority to recruit checked before a vacancy is published.

02

Clear job conditions

Role, location, pay, hours, contract terms and requirements made clear before application.

03

Fair candidate access

No charge to register interest. No selling jobs, visa promises or misleading guarantees.

04

Lawful international route

International hiring is assessed case by case with qualified Turkish legal and licensed recruitment partners.

05

Evidence and accountability

We aim to record employer authorisation, offer milestones, candidate acknowledgement and onboarding outcomes.

06

Privacy by design

Collect only necessary information, protect access and honour applicable data rights.
